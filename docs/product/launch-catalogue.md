# Launch catalogue

This is the editable controlled-launch catalogue for KopiContext. It implements the initial 25-Topic direction in [PRD 0005](../prd/0005-topic-discovery-analytics-and-sustainability.md). Inclusion means a Topic is a prioritised candidate for a source-backed Briefing; it does not imply that a Briefing has been approved or published.

The editorial lead may add, merge, rename, defer, or retire Topics through the Editorial Workflow. Agents may propose changes with confidence and rationale but cannot change this canonical catalogue themselves.

## Civic understanding

1. **How Singapore's Government Works** — first Briefing; a durable, neutral explanation of institutions and how they relate.
2. **Parliament and Elections** — legislative process and electoral mechanics; no candidate, party, or current-affairs analysis in the MVP.
3. **HDB and Public Housing** — the housing system, its trade-offs, and the vocabulary people use.
4. **CPF** — Singapore's compulsory savings framework and its everyday relevance.
5. **Digital Government** — how public digital services and digital identity shape daily interactions.

## Economy, business, and region

6. **Singapore's Economy** — the economic model, major sectors, and important terms.
7. **Trade and the Port** — Singapore's role in maritime trade and logistics.
8. **Startups and Venture Capital** — the local ecosystem, funding stages, and practical context.
9. **ASEAN** — the regional grouping and its relevance to Singapore.
10. **Global Supply Chains** — the systems connecting manufacturing, shipping, trade, and prices.
11. **Regional Geopolitics as Durable Background** — neutral structural context for regional conversations, not live political analysis.

## Technology and infrastructure

12. **Generative AI** — core concepts, capabilities, limits, and social implications.
13. **Semiconductors** — how chips are made, why they matter, and Singapore's role.
14. **Cybersecurity** — common risks, defensive concepts, and why they affect ordinary organisations.
15. **Data Centres** — their role in digital infrastructure and their local resource trade-offs.
16. **Energy Transition** — electricity, decarbonisation, and regional energy connections.
17. **The Green Plan and Climate Adaptation** — Singapore's sustainability framework and adaptation context.

## Everyday Singapore

18. **Public Transport** — how the network works and the terms people use when discussing it.
19. **COE and Road Pricing** — vehicle ownership controls, congestion management, and trade-offs.
20. **Ageing in Singapore** — demographic change, care, work, and community implications.
21. **Hawker Culture** — the social, economic, and cultural role of hawker centres.
22. **Singapore's Multicultural Festivals** — shared context for major traditions and observances.
23. **Local Arts** — the creative ecosystem, institutions, and ways into cultural conversation.

## Sport and shared culture

24. **Formula 1 Singapore** — the race's local significance, event vocabulary, and trade-offs.
25. **Football in Singapore** — the local game, institutions, and regional context.

## Catalogue rules

- Every Topic must use the [Briefing Template](../prd/0002-briefing-template-and-reader-experience.md) before it can be published.
- Civic Topics require primary official Sources where available; contested, interpretive, and current Claims need stronger corroboration.
- Current developments belong in separate Current Updates, never inside an evergreen Briefing as if they were stable context.
- Topic priority is informed by reader demand, conversation value, freshness, Source availability, and editorial judgement as defined in [PRD 0005](../prd/0005-topic-discovery-analytics-and-sustainability.md).
