## One-sentence explanation

CPF is Singapore’s compulsory social-security savings system: eligible working Singapore citizens and permanent residents generally receive employer and employee contributions for purposes including housing, healthcare and retirement. [\[S1\]](#s1) [\[S2\]](#s2)

## 30-second overview

CPF is not one ordinary bank account, and it is not only a retirement scheme. CPF Board describes it as a social-security system that supports retirement while also addressing healthcare, home ownership, family protection and asset enhancement. [\[S1\]](#s1)

While working, members and employers make monthly CPF contributions subject to the applicable rules. Savings sit in accounts with different purposes. [\[S1\]](#s1) [\[S2\]](#s2) CPF LIFE is a separate national longevity-insurance annuity scheme that can provide monthly payouts for as long as a member lives, subject to its rules. [\[S2\]](#s2) [\[S3\]](#s3)

## The five-minute explanation

### CPF connects several life needs

CPF Board describes CPF as a social-security system. Its purpose reaches beyond retirement to healthcare, home ownership, family protection and asset enhancement. [\[S1\]](#s1) That is why a conversation about CPF can arise from a payslip, a home, a medical bill or retirement planning.

The useful first question is: **which purpose or account is involved?** A general statement about CPF can be misleading if it ignores the relevant account, the member’s situation or a current rule.

### Accounts have different purposes

The Ordinary Account is used for permitted housing uses and other purposes under CPF rules. MediSave is for healthcare and approved medical-insurance uses. [\[S1\]](#s1) CPF Board’s overview also states that a Retirement Account is created at age 55 and the Special Account is closed at that point. [\[S2\]](#s2)

These descriptions do not make CPF savings freely interchangeable cash. They are a map of account purposes. Current transfer, use, withdrawal, interest and allocation rules need direct, dated checking with CPF Board.

### CPF LIFE is different from an account

CPF LIFE is about retirement income, not simply the label on an account. CPF Board describes CPF LIFE as a national longevity-insurance annuity scheme, providing monthly payouts for as long as a member lives, subject to the scheme’s rules. [\[S2\]](#s2) [\[S3\]](#s3)

The distinction matters because a reader might otherwise mix up a balance, an account purpose, a contribution rate and a retirement payout. They are related, but they are not the same thing.

### A practical example: asking a clearer CPF question

1. A working person sees CPF contributions on a payslip. Contributions are made monthly under the applicable rules. [\[S1\]](#s1)
2. Before acting, they identify whether the question concerns housing, healthcare or retirement savings.
3. If it concerns an account, they check the purpose of that account and the current CPF Board rule. [\[S1\]](#s1) [\[S2\]](#s2)
4. If it concerns retirement income, they treat CPF LIFE as a separate scheme and check its current information. [\[S3\]](#s3)

This is an orientation example, not advice to contribute, withdraw, invest, buy a home or choose a CPF LIFE plan.

## Why people care

CPF affects conversations about pay, a home, healthcare and retirement. The framework makes those conversations less confusing: **is this about contributions, an account purpose, or retirement income?** That question does not answer a person’s entitlement, but it helps find the right official information.

## Singapore / Southeast Asia angle

This Briefing explains Singapore’s CPF framework. It does not compare pension or social-security systems across countries, or make a claim about which system is better. Such comparison needs matching definitions, current rules and careful evidence.

## Discussion points, not positions

Useful questions for a conversation:

- Is this about a contribution, an account purpose, a withdrawal condition or retirement income?
- Is a quoted rate, limit, interest figure or payout current, and what qualifying context applies?
- Does the question need a personal calculation, a current CPF Board tool or qualified financial advice?
- Are people mixing up an account balance with CPF LIFE or a specific entitlement?

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **“CPF is only a retirement scheme.”** CPF Board also describes roles in healthcare, home ownership, family protection and asset enhancement. [\[S1\]](#s1)
- **“All CPF savings work like one cash account.”** Accounts have different stated purposes. [\[S1\]](#s1)
- **“CPF LIFE is simply another account.”** CPF LIFE is a separate longevity-insurance annuity scheme. [\[S2\]](#s2) [\[S3\]](#s3)
- **“A rate, limit or payout is evergreen.”** These values and rules need an effective date, qualifying context and current official source.
- **“This is financial advice.”** It is not; individual circumstances and current rules matter.
