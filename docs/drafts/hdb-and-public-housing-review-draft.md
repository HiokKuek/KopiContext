## One-sentence explanation

Public housing in Singapore is planned and delivered through the Housing & Development Board (HDB), with distinct journeys for new flats, resale flats, rental and managing a home. [\[S1\]](#s1) [\[S2\]](#s2)

## 30-second overview

HDB is a statutory board under the Ministry of National Development. Its published activities include selling and renting flats, upgrading and redeveloping estates, and mortgage loans for eligible purchasers under public-housing schemes. [\[S1\]](#s1)

HDB describes its role as creating homes and towns where communities thrive. [\[S2\]](#s2) A new-flat purchase, a resale purchase and managing a home are separate service journeys. [\[S3\]](#s3)

## The five-minute explanation

### HDB is more than a flat seller

HDB is a statutory board under the Ministry of National Development. Its financial statements describe activities that include selling and renting flats, upgrading and redeveloping older estates, and providing mortgage loans for eligible purchasers within public-housing schemes. [\[S1\]](#s1)

That broader role explains why conversations about HDB can cover a home, supply, a town, an estate, a resale transaction or a service rule. They are connected, but they are not the same question.

### New-flat and resale routes are not interchangeable

The **new-flat route** refers to HDB’s processes for new flats, such as Build-To-Order, Sale of Balance Flats and Open Booking of Flats. Current options, eligibility and availability need direct checking on HDB’s live pages. [\[S3\]](#s3)

The **resale route** concerns an existing HDB flat changing hands. HDB sets out planning, getting an Option to Purchase, submitting a resale application and completing the transaction. [\[S4\]](#s4) Do not assume the rules, timeline, grants or financing choices from one route apply to the other.

### Housing is a system with changing rules

The evergreen point is that there are distinct service paths. Income ceilings, grants, loan conditions, allocation exercises, occupation rules, resale prices and available units change. Show them only with an effective date, direct official source and qualifying context—not as permanent facts in this baseline.

### A practical example: choosing the right question first

1. A household distinguishes a new-flat route from a resale route, rather than treating “buying an HDB flat” as one identical process. [\[S3\]](#s3) [\[S4\]](#s4)
2. If it considers resale, it follows HDB’s published stages: plan, obtain an Option to Purchase, submit an application and complete the purchase. [\[S4\]](#s4)
3. It checks current HDB information for eligibility, grant, loan or availability questions rather than relying on a general explainer.
4. After purchase, home ownership and estate matters continue to have their own service journey. [\[S3\]](#s3)

This is an orientation example, not housing, financial or legal advice.

## Why people care

Housing conversations are often about more than a price. They can involve a home, a neighbourhood, family arrangements, a resale process, public supply or a changing rule. Start with the route and stage: **is this about a new flat, a resale flat, or managing a home?**

## Singapore / Southeast Asia angle

This Briefing explains Singapore’s public-housing system and HDB’s roles. It does not compare ownership rates, affordability, housing models or policies with other countries. Such comparisons need matching definitions and dated evidence.

## Discussion points, not positions

Useful questions for a conversation:

- Is the issue about homes and towns, a new-flat route, a resale transaction or managing a home?
- Is a statement durable context or a current rule, price, grant or supply figure?
- What personal circumstances affect the answer, and does the person need a current official tool or professional advice?
- Are people using “HDB” to mean the statutory board, a flat, an estate or a housing policy?

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **“HDB only sells flats.”** Its official activities also cover rental, upgrading, redevelopment and eligible public-housing mortgage loans. [\[S1\]](#s1)
- **“A new-flat and resale purchase follow the same process.”** HDB presents distinct routes; resale has its own stated stages. [\[S3\]](#s3) [\[S4\]](#s4)
- **“A resale price or grant is a permanent fact.”** Prices, grants and rules are current information requiring a date and source.
- **“This tells me if I qualify or what I should buy.”** It does not; use current HDB information and qualified advice for personal decisions.
