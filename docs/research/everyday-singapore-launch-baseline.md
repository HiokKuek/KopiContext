# Everyday Singapore: baseline research pack

**Prepared:** 7 August 2026  
**For:** the six Everyday Singapore Topics in the [launch content inventory](../product/launch-content-inventory.md).  
**Status:** research leads only — not accepted Sources, Claims, or publishable Briefings.

This is a source-backed starting point for six controlled-launch Briefings. It supplies reusable mental models, narrow candidate Claims, and [Briefing Template v1](../prd/0002-briefing-template-and-reader-experience.md) shapes. It never substitutes for Source Submission, source acceptance, Claim acceptance, or editorial approval.

## How to use this pack

1. Retrieve every linked page again in a Source Submission. Capture the exact passage, publication/update date, rights and access date.
2. Turn each candidate into one narrow Claim backed by an editor-accepted Source. Links here are research leads, not accepted evidence.
3. Put changing numbers and service details in a **Current Update**: fares, routes, quotas, premiums, ERP rates, service changes, festival dates, events, programme eligibility, statistics and grants.
4. Use neutral explanation. An agency's stated purpose is not proof that a policy produces the same outcome for every person.

All URLs below were accessed on **2026-08-07**. First-party owners are preferred: LTA, PTC, MOH, SingStat, NEA, NHB, MOM and NAC. UNESCO is used only for the international heritage inscription it owns. These are article-local terms; do not add them to `CONTEXT.md` merely because they occur in a Briefing.

## 1. Public Transport

### Reader mental model

```text
Walk / wheel / cycle → bus and rail network → interchange / destination
         │                     │
    access and links     routes, services and fares
         └──── planning, regulation and operations ────┘
```

Public transport is a connected journey system, not simply a train or bus. Active travel helps a person reach a stop; buses and rail carry different parts of a journey; interchanges make connections possible.

### Durable candidate Claims

| Candidate | Primary support | Editorial boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Singapore's public-transport system is centred on rail and bus networks; LTA plans, develops and maintains the system. | [LTA, *Public Transport System*](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/who_we_are/our_work/public_transport_system.html) | Attribute roles to LTA; do not imply all places have the same access or reliability. |
| LTA has builder and regulator roles for rail, while rail operators provide rail services under the New Rail Financing Framework. | [LTA, *Rail*](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/who_we_are/our_work/public_transport_system/rail.html) | Verify current framework wording and operators before publishing details. |
| In the bus network, trunk services connect neighbourhoods and towns while feeder services link MRT stations/interchanges with nearby areas. | [LTA, *Bus Network*](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/getting_around/public_transport/bus_network.html) | This is a simple network map, not a route recommendation. |
| LTA frames walking and cycling as complementary to public transport in a car-lite transport system. | [LTA, *Getting Around*](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/getting_around.html) | Present it as LTA's planning framing, not a claim about every trip. |
| PTC regulates public-transport fares and reviews fare adjustments within its statutory role. | [PTC, *About us*](https://www.ptc.gov.sg/about-us) | Current fares, concessions and decisions must be rechecked directly with PTC. |

### Template v1-oriented outline

1. **One-sentence answer:** Public transport is the shared network of buses, trains, walking/cycling links and interchanges that helps people make everyday journeys.
2. **30-second overview:** access → ride → transfer → last leg; show rail, bus and active-mobility cards.
3. **Five-minute explanation:** LTA, operators and PTC; rail, trunk, feeder, interchange, fare and service frequency; use a fictional home → feeder bus → MRT → walk journey.
4. **Why people care:** access to work, school, care and social life; public conversations include affordability, coverage, reliability, safety and accessibility.
5. **Questions / mistakes:** Ask which journey leg is at issue. Do not confuse an announced project with a current service improvement.

**Freshness:** policy/service, at least quarterly. Routes, fares, projects, operator lists, service levels, ridership and disruption notices are dated.

## 2. COE and Road Pricing

### Reader mental model

```text
Limited road space
   ├── COE: vehicle registration / ownership for a period
   └── ERP: selected-road use at selected times
        different moments, different tools
```

The important distinction is timing. COE concerns registering and owning a vehicle for a defined period; ERP concerns use of selected roads at selected times. Neither is simply a generic “car tax”.

### Durable candidate Claims

| Candidate | Primary support | Editorial boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A COE gives the right to own and use a vehicle in Singapore for 10 years; a bidder needs the relevant COE category to register a vehicle. | [OneMotoring/LTA, *Certificate of Entitlement*](https://onemotoring.lta.gov.sg/content/onemotoring/home/buying/upfront-vehicle-costs/certificate-of-entitlement--coe-.html) | Confirm duration, categories and exceptions against the current page. Do not give bidding advice. |
| The Vehicle Quota System controls new vehicle registrations in the context of limited road space; COEs are allocated by open bidding and quota is announced before each exercise. | [OneMotoring/LTA, *COE*](https://onemotoring.lta.gov.sg/content/onemotoring/home/buying/upfront-vehicle-costs/certificate-of-entitlement--coe-.html) | Premiums, quota numbers, calendar and rules are dated information. |
| ERP charges for use of selected roads at congested times as a way to manage congestion. | [LTA, *Roads*](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/who_we_are/our_work/road.html) | Attribute the purpose to LTA; do not make it a route/payment instruction. |
| COE and ERP address different moments: vehicle entry/ownership and road demand at a particular time/place. | [LTA COE](https://onemotoring.lta.gov.sg/content/onemotoring/home/buying/upfront-vehicle-costs/certificate-of-entitlement--coe-.html) and [LTA ERP](https://onemotoring.lta.gov.sg/content/onemotoring/home/driving/ERP.html) | Pedagogical comparison from official descriptions; do not claim identical effects. |
| ERP is a pay-as-you-use tool intended to influence mode, route or time of travel. | [MOT, *How ERP works as a speed booster*](https://www.mot.gov.sg/news-resources/resources/how-erp-works-as-a-speed-booster/) | Keep current rates, gantries, OBU rollout and future arrangements in updates. |

### Template v1-oriented outline

1. **One-sentence answer:** COE manages vehicle registration; ERP manages demand for road space on selected roads at selected times.
2. **30-second overview:** an equal COE-versus-ERP comparison before quota, bidding, premium and charging terms.
3. **Five-minute explanation:** trace deciding to register a vehicle, then choosing a peak-time route; add the broader transport alternatives map.
4. **Why people care:** road space, travel time, household costs, freight, accessibility and alternatives are all part of the public discussion.
5. **Questions / mistakes:** “Is this ownership or road use?” COE premium and ERP rate are not evergreen and neither is every car-related cost.

**Freshness:** policy/service, at least quarterly and after rule, quota, category, rate, charging-location or payment-system changes.

## 3. Ageing in Singapore

### Reader mental model

```text
People age across a life course
  ↓
health and prevention • home and community • work • care and support
  ↓
individuals, families, communities, employers and public systems all matter
```

Ageing is a population change and a lived experience, not a single service or problem. It touches health, work, housing, relationships, participation and care; older people do not form one uniform group.

### Durable candidate Claims

| Candidate | Primary support | Editorial boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MOH describes Age Well SG as helping seniors stay active, socially connected and receive care within their communities. | [MOH, *Ageing in the community*](https://www.moh.gov.sg/ageing-well/ageing-in-the-community/) | Attribute programme aims to MOH. Eligibility, locations and service details require fresh checking. |
| MOH's ageing-well resources span community ageing, care services, caregiving, advance care planning and palliative care. | [MOH, *Ageing well*](https://www.moh.gov.sg/ageing-well/) | Use this as a system map, not as a claim that every person needs each service. |
| MOH says Community Care Apartments combine housing with adjustable care services to support independent living as needs change. | [MOH, *Ageing in the community*](https://www.moh.gov.sg/ageing-well/ageing-in-the-community/) | This is not care-placement, medical or financial advice. Check current availability directly. |
| Official demographic data can orient the reader, but population shares, counts and projections need their reference year and definitions. | [MOH, *Population and Vital Statistics*](https://www.moh.gov.sg/others/resources-and-statistics/population-and-vital-statistics/) and [SingStat, *Elderly, youth and gender profile*](https://www.singstat.gov.sg/find-data/search-by-theme/population/elderly-youth-and-gender-profile/latest-data) | All numbers are dated; never use a projection as an evergreen result. |
| AIC helps people navigate care services and support alongside healthcare and community partners. | [AIC, *About us*](https://www.aic.sg/about-us/) | Verify current pathways live and do not provide individual care advice. |

### Template v1-oriented outline

1. **One-sentence answer:** Ageing in Singapore is the shared work of helping people live well as they grow older, across health, homes, work, community and care.
2. **30-second overview:** use the life-course map and distinguish population ageing from a person's own experience.
3. **Five-minute explanation:** active ageing, preventive health, caregiving, community support, housing and care; follow a fictional changing-needs example without advising on eligibility.
4. **Why people care:** families, workplaces, neighbourhoods, homes, transport and public resources all feature in conversation.
5. **Questions / mistakes:** “Which part of ageing is being discussed?” Do not equate ageing with illness, dependency or one financial situation.

**Freshness:** quarterly. Population data, programme rules, subsidies, care availability and health guidance are current information.

## 4. Hawker Culture

### Reader mental model

```text
Hawkers' skills and food traditions
        + shared centres where people eat and meet
        + livelihoods, hygiene, management and succession
        = a living culture, not only a list of dishes
```

Hawker culture includes people, food, knowledge, shared spaces and everyday practice. A hawker centre is one setting where this culture is lived; a stall review is not the whole system.

### Durable candidate Claims

| Candidate | Primary support | Editorial boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NEA describes hawker centres as community spaces where people from different walks of life eat and interact. | [NEA, *Managing hawker centres and markets*](https://www.nea.gov.sg/our-services/hawker-management) | Attribute this description to NEA; do not claim every centre is the same. |
| Hawker Culture in Singapore was inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2020. | [UNESCO, inscription record](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/hawker-culture-in-singapore-community-dining-and-culinary-practices-in-a-multicultural-urban-context-01539) and [NEA, *Hawker Culture*](https://www.nea.gov.sg/our-services/hawker-management/programmes-and-grants/hawker-culture) | Inscription is heritage recognition, not a quality rating for every stall. |
| NEA's description includes hawkers' knowledge and culinary skills, food, space and community. | [NEA, *Hawker Culture*](https://www.nea.gov.sg/our-services/hawker-management/programmes-and-grants/hawker-culture) | Avoid asserting one fixed origin or “authentic” form for a dish without a suitable heritage source. |
| NEA manages specified markets and hawker centres and regulates related tenancy and public-health matters. | [NEA, *Hawker management*](https://www.nea.gov.sg/our-services/hawker-management) | Management, operators, rents and tenancy conditions are operational details, not baseline facts. |
| Hawker-culture conversation can include affordability, livelihoods, succession, hygiene and shared public space. | [NEA, *Hawker Culture*](https://www.nea.gov.sg/our-services/hawker-management/programmes-and-grants/hawker-culture) | Frame as questions/trade-offs, not proof that a policy has solved them. |

### Template v1-oriented outline

1. **One-sentence answer:** Hawker culture is the living combination of food, hawkers' skills and shared centres where people eat and connect.
2. **30-second overview:** people + food + shared space + conditions that keep a culture going; explain the UNESCO inscription simply.
3. **Five-minute explanation:** hawker, hawker centre, market, culinary practice and intangible heritage; use a fictional visit, not a stall list.
4. **Why people care:** meals, community space, livelihoods, tradition, affordability and food safety all occur in public discussion.
5. **Questions / mistakes:** Ask whether the subject is food, heritage, livelihood or management. Do not generalise from one queue, review or price.

**Freshness:** structural, review annually. Management programmes, tenders, closures, stallholders, menus and prices are all dated.

## 5. Singapore's Multicultural Festivals

### Reader mental model

```text
Faiths, communities and family traditions
       ↓
observances with their own meanings and practices
       ↓
public-holiday calendar, community events and shared civic space
```

Singapore's festival calendar is not a single combined tradition. Observances rooted in different communities and faiths can share public space while retaining their own meanings. A public event and a religious observance can overlap without being the same thing.

### Durable candidate Claims

| Candidate | Primary support | Editorial boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Singapore's gazetted public-holiday list includes celebrations associated with more than one religious and cultural tradition, alongside civic and secular holidays. | [MOM, public-holiday FAQ](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/public-holidays/how-many-days-of-public-holidays-is-an-employee-entitled-to-in-a-year) | Recheck annually; the list does not capture every festival observed in Singapore. |
| People in Singapore observe diverse social practices, rituals and festive events rooted in their communities and religions. | [NHB Roots, *Intangible Cultural Heritage in Singapore*](https://www.roots.gov.sg/-/media/Roots/ich/ich-booklet/ich-booklet.ashx) | Do not use an overview to generalise an individual's belief or practice. |
| Some festival dates follow lunar, religious or other calendars, and may therefore differ each Gregorian year. | [NHB Roots, *Festivals in Singapore*](https://www.roots.gov.sg/stories-landing/stories/festivals-in-singapore/festivals-in-singapore) | Cite a specific source for the relevant calendar; confirm all dates annually. |
| A respectful explanation distinguishes a faith's observance from public decorations, markets or performances associated with it. | [NHB Roots, *Festivals in Singapore*](https://www.roots.gov.sg/stories-landing/stories/festivals-in-singapore/festivals-in-singapore) | Editorial reading habit; do not reduce a festival to food, costume or spectacle. |
| A public holiday is an employment-law concept with specific entitlements, not a complete statement of an observance's cultural or religious significance. | [MOM, *Public Holidays for 2026*](https://www.mom.gov.sg/newsroom/press-releases/2025/0616-public-holidays-for-2026) | Use current MOM guidance for legal entitlements. |

### Template v1-oriented outline

1. **One-sentence answer:** Singapore's multicultural festivals are different community and faith observances that share public space but retain their own meanings, customs and calendars.
2. **30-second overview:** three layers: observance, family/community practice, public-calendar/event context.
3. **Five-minute explanation:** selected carefully sourced examples such as Hari Raya, Deepavali, Vesak Day, Chinese New Year or Christmas; practices vary within and across communities.
4. **Why people care:** school, work, family visits, neighbourhood activity, language and public holidays shape everyday encounters.
5. **Questions / mistakes:** Ask what an occasion means to observers and whether the date is current. Do not assume shared practice by ethnicity.

**Freshness:** seasonal/event, annually and before each relevant season. Holiday dates, public treatment, event lists and venue arrangements are dated.

## 6. Local Arts

### Reader mental model

```text
Artists and arts workers ↔ groups, venues, producers and educators
          ↓                              ↓
       create / present work ↔ audiences, participants and communities
          ↓
    public, private and community support can enable activity
```

Local arts are an ecosystem, not one institution or event calendar. It includes practitioners, organisations, venues, audiences, participants, educators and supporters across art forms. Funding is one support mechanism, not a measure of artistic worth or a guarantee of access.

### Durable candidate Claims

| Candidate | Primary support | Editorial boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NAC describes its role as supporting Singapore's arts sector through work with artists, audiences, partnerships, research and capability development. | [NAC, *Management and functions*](https://www.nac.gov.sg/about-us/management/) | Attribute this to NAC; do not imply NAC runs every arts activity. |
| Singapore's arts activity can include performances, exhibitions, publications, participatory projects and festivals across art forms. | [NAC, *Presentation and Participation Grant guidelines*](https://www.nac.gov.sg/docs/default-source/support-files/funding-and-schemes/presentation-and-participation-grant/p-p-guidelines.pdf?sfvrsn=d357c4b7_16) | Grant categories are evidence of supported work, not a complete definition of art or an eligibility promise. |
| Arts participation can include viewing, listening, reading, learning or co-creating, depending on the activity. | [NAC, *Presentation and Participation Grant guidelines*](https://www.nac.gov.sg/docs/default-source/support-files/funding-and-schemes/presentation-and-participation-grant/p-p-guidelines.pdf?sfvrsn=d357c4b7_16) | Examples only: not all participation is ticketed or equally accessible. |
| NAC publishes Singapore Cultural Statistics as a dated annual update on arts and cultural activity. | [NAC, *Singapore Cultural Statistics*](https://www.nac.gov.sg/resources/research/others/singapore-cultural-statistics) | Every figure needs its edition, definition and reference period. |
| Our SG Arts Plan is a time-bounded 2023–2027 strategy that expresses NAC's stated direction, not a permanent outcome. | [NAC, *Our SG Arts Plan*](https://www.nac.gov.sg/about-us/oursgartsplan) | Verify successor planning after 2027. |

### Template v1-oriented outline

1. **One-sentence answer:** Local arts are the work and shared experiences made possible by artists, arts workers, organisations, venues, audiences and communities in Singapore.
2. **30-second overview:** make/present work ↔ participate/respond; support and infrastructure surround the work rather than sit above it.
3. **Five-minute explanation:** artist, producer, venue, presenter, audience, participant, grant and cultural statistic; trace a fictional work to an audience encounter.
4. **Why people care:** arts offer ways to encounter languages, stories, histories, identities and ideas, and are also a field of work and learning.
5. **Questions / mistakes:** Ask whose work, audience or support is under discussion. Do not equate funding with endorsement or popularity with value.

**Freshness:** data/industry, quarterly. Grant rules, calls, strategies, programmes, statistics, venue activity and event calendars are dated.
