# KopiContext

KopiContext gives English-speaking Singaporeans concise, source-backed context so they can join a conversation thoughtfully. It is a conversation-briefing product, not a news publisher or an encyclopedia.

## Language

**Topic**:
A broad subject that users want to understand and that can have a published briefing, related entities, events, and updates.
_Avoid_: article, story

**Briefing**:
The structured, evergreen explanation that gives a person enough context to participate in a conversation about a Topic.
_Avoid_: article, post, report

**Briefing Template**:
The versioned editorial structure that specifies the required sections and evidence expectations of a Briefing.
_Avoid_: article format, page layout

**Current Update**:
A dated development associated with a Topic, Entity, or Event and kept visibly separate from its evergreen Briefing.
_Avoid_: news, latest information

**Subtopic**:
A narrower concept within a Topic that is proposed or linked to make a Briefing easier to navigate.
_Avoid_: category, child article

**Source**:
An external, traceable reference that supports one or more Claims.
_Avoid_: citation, reference material

**Claim**:
A factual statement in KopiContext content that is supported by one or more Sources.
_Avoid_: fact

**Editor-authored Claim**:
A Claim written directly by the editor and linked to one or more Accepted Sources. It has the same evidence and publication requirements as an agent-suggested Claim; only its origin differs.
_Avoid_: uncited manual note

**Editorial Approval**:
The sole editor's explicit decision to publish content after agent-assisted preparation and verification.
_Avoid_: automatic publishing

**Editorial Workflow**:
The review path through which drafts and proposed Claims are verified, approved, published, archived, or returned for further work.
_Avoid_: publishing pipeline

**Source Submission**:
Material supplied to the editorial workflow for assessment, such as a transcript, URL, or document; it is not a Source until the editor accepts it as support for Claims.
_Avoid_: source, article upload
